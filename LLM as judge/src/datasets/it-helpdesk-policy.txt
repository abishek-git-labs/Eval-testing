Northstar IT Helpdesk Policy (Fictional Training Example)

Scope
This policy applies to Northstar employees using company-managed laptops.

Support hours
The helpdesk operates Monday through Friday, 09:00-17:00 UTC.
The policy does not specify weekend support arrangements.

Opening a ticket
Submit a ticket through the employee support portal. Include the asset ID,
a description of the issue, and the exact error message if one is shown.
This policy does not provide a support email address or telephone number.

Priorities and initial response targets
P1: A company-wide outage has an initial response target of 30 minutes.
P2: An issue blocking one employee's work has an initial response target
of 4 business hours.
P3: A non-blocking request has an initial response target of 2 business days.
These targets are measured during support hours. They are not guarantees
of resolution time.

Password resets
Employees can use the self-service password portal. If it is unavailable,
contact the helpdesk through the support portal. Helpdesk-assisted resets
require identity verification. Support never asks for a password or MFA code.

Software installation
Employees may install applications listed in the approved software catalog.
Applications not in the catalog require IT approval before installation.
Local administrator access is not granted automatically.

Loan laptops
An employee whose company laptop needs repair may request a loan laptop.
Issue of a loan laptop depends on availability and manager approval.
Same-day availability is not guaranteed.

Lost or stolen devices
Report a lost or stolen company laptop immediately through the security
incident form. Do not wait until the next working day.

Personal devices
The helpdesk does not repair personal devices. This policy does not specify
whether personal devices may access company email.
