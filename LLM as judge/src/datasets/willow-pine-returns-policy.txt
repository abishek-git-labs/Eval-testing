Willow & Pine — Returns and Refunds Policy

This policy applies to purchases from the fictional Willow & Pine online store.

Return period
You may request a return within 30 calendar days of delivery.

Condition
Returned items must be unused and in their original packaging.

Items that cannot be returned
Items marked Final Sale and digital products are not eligible for return.

Return shipping
For a standard return, the customer pays the return shipping cost. Original shipping charges are not refunded.

Damaged or defective items
Contact Willow & Pine Support within 7 calendar days of delivery and include your order number and a description of the issue. Willow & Pine will provide a prepaid return label. You may choose a replacement or a full refund, including the original shipping charge.

Refund timing
After an eligible returned item is received and inspected, the refund is sent to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. Your bank or card provider may take additional time to display it.

Gift returns
Eligible gifts may be returned for store credit. The original purchaser is not notified.

For help with a return, contact Willow & Pine Support with your order number.
